Be easy to find on Google

When someone nearby searches for a business like yours, Google shows a profile with your name, photos, reviews, opening hours and a map. I set it up properly and make sure it matches your website.

I can’t promise a number one ranking, and nobody honestly can. What I can do is give you a complete, accurate profile and a fast, clear website, which is what gives you the best chance. The profile stays in your name. I only ask for manager access, and you can remove it at any time.

What I set up for you

  • Claim and verify your business profile
  • Choose the right main and extra categories
  • Add your address or service area, phone number and opening hours
  • List your services or products in plain words
  • Upload clear photos of your shop, work and team
  • Create a short link customers can use to leave a review
  • Link it to your website, with call and WhatsApp buttons
  • Show you how to post updates and reply to reviews

Your website uses the same name, address and phone number as your profile, and includes details Google can read, so the two support each other.

Questions clients often ask

How much does a website cost?

Websites start from ₹5,999, with hosting and domain included. The final price depends on the pages and features you need. After a short chat, I give you a fixed quote in writing, so you know the cost before any work starts.

How long does it take?

A small business website usually takes a few weeks. I confirm the timeline in your quote.

What do you need from me?

Your business name and contact details, a list of what you offer, and any logo or photos you have. If you don’t have photos yet, I’ll tell you what to take.

I have a website. Do I still need a Google Business Profile?

For a local business, yes. It’s often the first thing people see when they search, and it works best together with your website.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Many customers browse on their phones, so I design for phones first and test on real devices.

Can you update it after launch?

Yes. Tell me what you want changed and we’ll agree the cost before I begin.
